“Microsoft AI adapts an explanation from bounded football evidence. Observations stay separate from interpretation, and source IDs let us inspect the records. If an answer fails our checks, PRO-VISION falls back to its computed explanation.”

“A fan gets a clear explanation. An analyst gets derived measurements and review questions. We don’t invent speed, defensive positions or chance quality when the data doesn’t support them.”

**0:58–1:12 — Evidence and pressure sensitivity**
“Click a source to replay it. Remove a pressure record to see how that signal changes. This is sensitivity analysis—not a prediction of what would have happened.”

“Language and player preferences shape the experience. The same evidence can produce a timed overlay with machine-readable references.”

**1:25–1:42 — Save memory, show shirt and simulated tap**
“A fan can save the moment, add their own note or photo, and download an interactive keepsake. The clothing preview demonstrates a future connection: this tap is simulated, and opens the saved replay.”

**1:42–1:50 — Close**
“PRO-VISION connects what happened, why it may matter, and what it means to each fan—with evidence they can explore.”
